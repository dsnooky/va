01 — THE BRAND

Clean skincare with a confident visual identity.

Luméa Skin is a fictional skincare brand focused on simple, everyday hydration and healthy-looking skin.

The visual identity was designed to feel clean, feminine, premium, and approachable while keeping the products as the main focus.

“Glow starts with hydration.”

LUMÉA SKIN

Pure Skin. Real Confidence.

02 — MARKETING GOAL

Turn product features into content customers notice.

Build Product Awareness Introduce the hydration-focused product line through attractive promotional visuals.
Highlight Benefits Communicate product benefits in a simple, easy-to-understand format.
Promote the Product Duo Use a limited-time promotional offer to encourage purchase interest.
Create Social Content Adapt the campaign into engaging Facebook and Instagram content.

Content Strategy

  • Product awareness
  • Product education
  • Benefits-focused content
  • Promotional content
  • Lifestyle storytelling
  • Social media engagement
03 — CAMPAIGN DESIGNS

One campaign. Five marketing visuals.

Each design was created to serve a different purpose within the product marketing campaign.

01 — Hero Product Ad

Glow Starts With Hydration

02 — Benefits Graphic

03 — Promotional Graphic

Glow Duo — Save 20%

A promotional visual designed to create urgency and encourage customers to shop.

04 — Product Showcase

05 — Social Media Post

Your 5-Minute Glow Routine

Lifestyle-oriented content designed for Facebook and Instagram audiences.

04 — VISUAL DIRECTION

Soft, premium and product-focused.

The campaign uses warm neutrals, soft blush tones, elegant typography, natural textures, and generous whitespace to create a premium skincare aesthetic.

Cream Blush Gold Minimal Feminine Natural

Design Principle

Keep the product visible, make the message easy to understand, and create enough visual emotion to make the audience stop scrolling.
