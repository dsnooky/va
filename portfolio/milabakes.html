01 — THE BRAND

A bakery brand built around warmth, freshness, and happiness.

Mila Bakes is a fictional small-batch bakery created around the idea that freshly baked treats can turn ordinary moments into something special.

The visual direction focuses on warm photography, soft bakery colors, appetizing product presentation, and friendly messaging that feels personal rather than overly corporate.

“Made fresh. Packed with love.”

02 — MARKETING GOAL

Make customers hungry before they even place an order.

Showcase the Products Present the cookies in an attractive and appetizing way.
Build Product Awareness Introduce the different cookie flavors and the Sweet Box offering.
Increase Order Interest Use bundles and promotional messaging to encourage customers to place an order.
Create Social Engagement Develop content that is visually appealing and easy to share on social media.

Content Strategy

  • Product showcase
  • Flavor education
  • Menu content
  • Bundle promotion
  • Limited-time offers
  • Lifestyle storytelling
03 — CAMPAIGN DESIGNS

A sweet campaign with five marketing touchpoints.

Each design was created for a different stage of the customer's journey—from discovering the product to deciding what to order.

01 — Hero Advertisement

A Little Box of Happiness

A product-focused advertisement designed to immediately create appetite and introduce the Sweet Box.

02 — Menu Graphic

Choose Your Favorite

A simple menu layout that makes flavors, options, and product choices easy to scan.

03 — Bundle Promotion

12 Cookies + Free Gift Box

A promotional graphic designed to increase perceived value and encourage larger orders.

04 — Limited Offer

Weekend Sweet Deal

A time-sensitive promotional design created to add urgency to the campaign.

05 — Social Media Content

Made Fresh. Packed With Love.

A warm lifestyle post designed to build emotional connection with the audience.

04 — CONTENT STRATEGY

Different posts. One consistent brand experience.

For a small bakery, every post should have a purpose. Instead of repeatedly posting product photos, the campaign uses different content angles to keep the audience interested.

The content moves between product awareness, education, promotion, urgency, and emotional storytelling.

Product Showcase Menu Promotion Urgency Lifestyle Engagement

The Marketing Idea

Don't just tell customers that the cookies taste good. Show them the product, give them a reason to order, make choosing easy, and create a reason to order today.
